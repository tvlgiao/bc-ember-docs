    The Theme Editor, section by section¶

    The editor is organised into nine sections. One of them, Ember Theme, holds everything about how the storefront looks. Another, Lookbook, configures the shoppable-scenes page. The remaining seven are i18n.* sections holding storefront wording, which BigCommerce fills from the theme's language files — most stores never open them.

    Dark Mode¶

    Ember Theme has a real dark mode, not an inverted filter. Both palettes are designed.

    Setting Effect
    Theme Mode System follows the visitor's operating system preference, Light and Dark force one. Default: System.
    Dark Toggle Shows a toggle button in the header so visitors can override whatever the default was.

    A visitor's choice is remembered in their browser and survives page loads. The theme sets the mode before the page paints, so there is no flash of the wrong palette on load.

    Colors¶

    Five colours drive the whole storefront. Every component derives from them, so you do not need to restyle pieces individually.

    Setting Used for
    Accent Highlights, sale flags, active states
    Primary Buttons, links, anything the shopper is meant to click
    Heading Headings
    Text Body copy
    Muted Secondary copy, captions, metadata

    Two more sit beside them: Accent text, the accent as used for text (often a shade darker than the accent itself, so it stays readable on white), and Button, the fill of the primary buttons.

    Dark Mode Colors repeats the same set for dark mode. Dark mode reads only these, so a colour changed under Colors does not carry over to it.

    Each variation ships a palette that meets WCAG AA contrast in both light and dark mode. If you change these values, check the result in both modes — a colour that reads well on white often fails on the dark surface.

    Primary button colour¶

    Primary buttons — Add to Cart, Checkout, Sign In, Apply — take their fill from Ember Theme → Button in light mode and Dark Mode Colors → Button in dark mode. Hover and pressed shades are mixed from it, so one colour is all you set.

    The standard Cornerstone controls under Buttons and icons → Primary action button still work, in light mode, and take over once you change them:

    Setting Counts as "not set" at When you change it
    Button background #1B1D21 Replaces the Button colour; hover and pressed shades are mixed from it
    Button background hover #333333 Replaces the mixed hover shade
    Button background active #000000 Replaces the mixed pressed shade

    Three values mean “use the theme’s colour”

    Those three values are what the theme ships with, and a control left at its shipped value is treated as untouched — so the theme's own Button colour paints the buttons. That means choosing exactly #1B1D21 for the background, #333333 for hover or #000000 for pressed changes nothing. If one of those is the colour you want, pick one a step away, such as #1B1D22 or #010101; nobody can see the difference. Button text color has no such rule and always applies. None of the Cornerstone button controls has a dark-mode twin: in dark mode, use Dark Mode Colors → Button.

    Layout¶

    Setting Options Default
    Background colour —
    Surface colour used for cards and panels —
    Border colour —
    Max Width 1200px, 1400px, 1600px 1400px
    Border Radius 0px, 8px, 14px, 20px 14px

    Border Radius applies globally — buttons, cards, inputs, images. 0px gives a hard-edged look; 20px is soft and rounded.

    Typography¶

    Fonts are chosen with the standard Cornerstone controls: Global → Body text and links → Body text font family and Global → Headings → Heading font family. The theme's heading scale adapts to whichever family you pick.

    Each variation ships a pair:

    Variation Body text Headings
    Home Plus Jakarta Sans Plus Jakarta Sans, at weights 700 and 800
    Fashion Jost Playfair Display
    Food DM Sans Fraunces

    Each font offered by those two controls loads every weight the theme draws it at — for body text, regular through extra-bold plus italic — so bold text comes from the real bold cut instead of being thickened by the browser. (The checkout's own font controls are separate and unchanged.)

    Upgrading from an earlier build? Pick your fonts again

    Earlier builds saved a font with a single weight, for example Google_Plus+Jakarta+Sans_400. A saved setting is kept when you upgrade, so a store that still has one of those loads that one weight only, and every bold heading, price and button label is faked by the browser. Open both font controls, select your font again and save; the setting is rewritten with every weight, for example Google_Plus+Jakarta+Sans_400,500,600,700,800,400italic. If the dropdown already shows your font, pick a different one first and then yours, so the editor registers a change.

    Announcement bar¶

    Up to three rotating messages across the top of every page. Leave a message empty to skip it; leave all three empty to hide the bar.

    Mega menu¶

    Two promotional slots inside the navigation dropdowns, each an image plus a link: one under Categories, one under Shop. There is also a banner image and text for the All Categories panel.

    Homepage¶

    Block Controls
    Hero pills The three short notes under the hero buttons: Built-in text (translated), Your text below (Pill 1 to Pill 3; an empty pill is hidden) or Hidden (since 1.1.28)
    Trust badges On/off, plus a title and a line of text for each of three badges
    Promo banner On/off, one wide banner image, three supporting images, and an option to repeat the trust badges beneath
    Flash sale On/off, source (Best Sellers, Featured, or New Arrivals), title, text, and an end date that drives the countdown
    Reviews On/off, an aggregate score and count, and three named quotes
    Newsletter On/off, title and text
    Brand story On/off
    Press bar On/off, a title, and five logo-plus-link slots

    Hero pills¶

    The built-in pills read "Free shipping", "30-day returns" and "Secure checkout", in the shopper's language. If your store charges shipping on some or all orders, or has a different returns window, set Hero pills to Your text below and reword them (for example "Free shipping on many pieces"), or choose Hidden. The choice applies to every hero slide, including slides built from Storefront > Carousel. On phones the pills are not shown.

    Hero focus on phones and tablets¶

    The hero photographs are wide, and below 1100px the hero box is much narrower than the picture, so the photo is cropped to fill it. Hero 1 Focus, Hero 2 Focus and Hero 3 Focus (phones & tablets), one per slide, choose which part of the width stays in view: 0% (left) keeps the left edge, 50% (centre) crops evenly from both sides, 100% (right) keeps the right edge, in steps of 10%. Set each one to where the subject of that slide stands. Desktop always shows the centred crop.

    Variation Hero 1 Hero 2 Hero 3
    Home 50% 50% 50%
    Fashion 80% 90% 90%
    Food 70% 80% 80%

    These are the values each variation ships with, chosen for its own photographs. A slide replaced through Page Builder (see Replacing the homepage hero) is your own widget, and its focus setting does not apply to it.

    Category pages¶

    The category header. The category image you set in BigCommerce sits in the header at the top of that category's page. Global → Products → Category header with an image picks how:

    Option Effect
    Banner: photo behind the title (default) The photo fills the header and the breadcrumb, title and description sit on it, over a shade that keeps the text readable
    Split: title beside the whole photo The title and description sit beside the image, which is shown whole (the Fashion style starts on Split)

    With Split:

    • From 600px wide, the title and description are on the left and the image on the right at a fixed height: 240px on desktops, 200px on tablets. The image keeps its own shape and is not cropped; a very wide image (wider than about 2:1) is shrunk to fit instead.
    • On phones the image sits under the title, 180px tall.
    • A category with no image, or no description, shows only what it has.

    Choosing a category image

    Any shape works, because nothing is cropped. A landscape image between 4:3 and 2:1 uses the space best. Upload it at least 1000px wide so it stays sharp on high-density screens.

    Filters. The Clear button in the filter sidebar appears only once a filter is set. A store without product filtering shows a compact Shop By Price block instead.

    Product pages¶

    Setting Effect
    Sticky ATC Bar Keeps an add-to-cart bar visible as the shopper scrolls past the buy box
    Q&A Tab Adds a questions tab to the product tabs
    Compare button outside category and brand pages Shows the compare button on product cards. Turned off, no card shows it, category and brand pages included; turned on, those two pages also need BigCommerce's own product comparison setting
    Show quickview button on product cards Shows the Quick View button on product cards. Turned off, no card shows it anywhere; turned on, it also shows on touch tablets 768px and wider, where it is always visible instead of appearing on hover. Touch phones never show it

    The share buttons under the product follow the social sharing buttons ticked in the control panel, in the same order, including Print and the Facebook Like button. Untick them all and the product page shows no share row.

    Cart¶

    Setting Effect
    Free Shipping ($) The threshold the shipping progress bar counts toward: 25, 50, 75, 100, 150, or 200
    Free Shipping Messages Shows the free-shipping amount wherever the theme mentions it: the top bar's default first message, the cart sheet, the product page, the cart page (progress card and "Free ship" badge) and the note under Free Shipping in the home promo banner. Turn it off to hide all of them (called Shipping Bar before 1.1.27, when it hid only the cart page card)
    Cross-sell Shows related products in the cart
    Trust Badges Shows the badges in the cart
    Recently Viewed Shows a recently-viewed strip, with a configurable item count

    Set the Free Shipping threshold to match the free-shipping rule you configured in Settings → Shipping. The theme does not read your shipping rules; it only draws the bar.

    Below the threshold, the cart page, the cart sheet that opens after Add to Cart, and the product page all say how much is still missing — "Add $12.00 more for free shipping" — in the shopper's currency. Once the cart reaches it, the shipping row reads FREE.

    If your store ships free on every order, or has no free-shipping offer, turn Free Shipping Messages off (since 1.1.27). A Free Shipping amount of 0 is treated the same way. With the messages off and the first top-bar message left empty, the top bar starts with its second message; to say "Free shipping on every order" there, type it into the first top-bar message.

    The cross-sell row on the cart page leaves out products that are already in the cart. If that leaves fewer products than the row holds, it shows the ones that remain.

    About page¶

    Settings for the page that uses the About template (since 1.1.25).

    Setting Effect
    Story photo (Image Manager file name) Your own Our Story photo: the file name of an image uploaded in Storefront > Image Manager. Empty: the theme's photo for your style.
    Story photo description (alt text) What a screen reader says for your photo.
    Stats Built-in text (translated), Your text below (the four number and label pairs) or Hidden.
    Values The same three choices for the six value cards (title and text each).
    Hero sentence and closing banner Built-in text (translated) or Your text below: Hero sentence, Banner title, Banner text, Button label and Button link (a page of your store, such as /shop-all/; since 1.1.26).

    With Your text below, an empty number or title hides that item, and the section disappears when all of them are empty; an empty hero sentence or banner field hides that piece. Details and examples: The other page templates.

    Payment icons¶

    Header and footer → Payment icons has one checkbox per payment method: American Express, Discover, Mastercard, PayPal, Visa, Amazon Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Klarna. The icons you tick show in the footer, the cart, the product page and the cart drawer. Show Apple Pay (new in 1.1.13) starts off, so turn it on if your store accepts Apple Pay.

    Prices hidden from guests¶

    BigCommerce can hide prices from shoppers who are not signed in. That is a store setting, not a theme one, and the theme follows it everywhere it prints a price. For a guest on such a store, Ember shows no price on:

    • product cards, wherever they appear
    • the product page, its bulk pricing table and its fixed shipping cost
    • the free-shipping progress bar in the cart (the threshold sentence stays, the bar and the amount still missing do not)
    • the structured data (JSON-LD) search engines read from the product page

    What this means for search results

    The theme leaves the whole offers block out of the structured data for guests, since an offer without a price is an error in Google's Rich Results test. Search engines crawl as guests, so Google has no price or availability to show in a rich result for your products. That is the trade-off of hiding prices from guests. Signed-in shoppers see every price as usual.
