    Ember Theme — Settings Reference¶

    Every setting the Theme Editor exposes, grouped the way the editor groups them. 421 settings across 9 sections.

    Every section except Ember Theme and Lookbook comes from Cornerstone and holds storefront wording and platform styling. They are listed for completeness, but a store that sells in one language rarely needs to touch them — BigCommerce fills them from the theme's language files.

    Section Settings
    Ember Theme 126
    Global 44
    Header and footer 35
    Homepage 3
    Products 46
    Buttons and icons 25
    Checkout page 68
    Payment buttons 33
    Lookbook 41

Pill 2 is also the cart page returns badge, which Hidden removes. With Your text or Hidden, the first built-in hero slide also drops its line about shipping and returns.
