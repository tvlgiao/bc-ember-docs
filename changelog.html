    Release Notes¶

    1.1.13 — 24 October 2026¶

    Adds one setting; nothing was removed or renamed, so installing over 1.1.12 keeps your configuration. The new Show Apple Pay setting starts off, so your payment icons look the same until you turn it on. The gift wrapping dialog's "choose a gift wrap" text is shorter in every language.

    New¶

    Payment icons¶

    • Apple Pay icon: turn on Theme Editor → Header and footer → Payment icons → Show Apple Pay to add an Apple Pay icon next to your other payment icons in the footer, the cart, the product page and the cart drawer. It is off by default.

    Fixes¶

    Cart¶

    • Gift wrap choice fits on phones: the "Choose gift wrap" text in the gift wrapping dialog fits in the box on phones in every language, instead of being cut off.

    Home page¶

    • Cleaner hero slide change: when the hero slides change, only the pictures crossfade; the old and new headings no longer overlap while the slide changes.

    Category and brand pages¶

    • No product lookups on listing pages: category and brand pages no longer ask the store for product data just to count products. A category's product count comes from the page itself; a brand's count shows when all of its products fit on one page. Brand logos in the Brands menu load when the menu is first opened instead of on every page.

    Breadcrumbs¶

    • Breadcrumbs on phones: the breadcrumb trail now shows on phones on product, category, brand, brands and web pages, on one line; long names in the middle shorten with an ellipsis so the current page stays readable. Hide breadcrumbs still removes it.

    1.1.12 — 23 October 2026¶

    No setting was added, removed or renamed, so installing over 1.1.11 keeps your configuration; there is nothing to configure.

    Fixes¶

    Dialogs¶

    • Close button lines up with the title: the close button in every dialog (gift wrapping, write a review, and every other one) sits on the same line as the dialog title instead of slightly below it, and stays beside the first line when a long title wraps.

    1.1.11 — 23 October 2026¶

    No setting was added, removed or renamed, so installing over 1.1.10 keeps your configuration; there is nothing to configure.

    Fixes¶

    Blog¶

    • Blog card photos: blog cards on the home page ("Latest from Our Blog") and the blog page show each post's photo in a 4:3 frame instead of a thin strip, so portrait photos are no longer cut down to a band. On the Food style's home page, where the blog cards sit side by side on wider screens, the photo still fills its column.

    Header¶

    • Switching currency with items in the cart: when a shopper picks another currency while their cart has items, the cart and the page change to that currency together in one step, and the switch no longer risks losing the cart.

    1.1.10 — 23 October 2026¶

    No setting was added, removed or renamed, so installing over 1.1.9 keeps your configuration; there is nothing to configure.

    Fixes¶

    Cart¶

    • Gift Certificate field in other currencies: the Gift Certificate field stays available when a shopper views prices in another currency but their cart is still in the store's default currency.

    1.1.9 — 23 October 2026¶

    No setting was added, removed or renamed, so installing over 1.1.8 keeps your configuration; there is nothing to configure. The promo code field in the cart now reads "Promo code", and the new gift certificate field reads "Certificate code" (stores that allow several promo codes per order).

    Fixes¶

    Cart¶

    • Gift Certificate row: on stores that allow several promo codes per order (BigCommerce's newer cart summary), the cart summary has its own Gift Certificate row, next to the promo code row. Shoppers select Add to open a code field and Apply to redeem it, the same way the promo code works. The row is hidden when the shopper is browsing in a currency where gift certificates can't be used. Stores on the classic cart summary keep the gift certificate field as it was.
    • Promo code field: the field is now labelled "Promo code". It still accepts a gift certificate code.
    • Currency after switching: after a shopper switches currency, the cart now uses the currency they picked instead of the one they were in before.
    • Quantity buttons: tapping the − and + buttons on the product page, quick view, cart and cart drawer no longer flashes the browser's blue highlight; the button shows a pressed state in your theme's accent colour. Keyboard users still see the focus outline.

    Header¶

    • Search bar corners: the header search bar follows your theme's corner radius instead of always being fully rounded; the Fashion style keeps square corners. The search box on phones follows the same setting.

    1.1.8 — 22 October 2026¶

    Adds one setting; nothing was removed or renamed, so installing over 1.1.7 keeps your configuration. The new Category header with an image setting starts on Banner (the Fashion style starts on Split). The gift card preview now labels its names "To" and "From".

    New¶

    Category pages¶

    • Category header banner: when a category has an image, the photo now sits behind the breadcrumb and title as a banner, with a shade that keeps the title easy to read. Choose the look in Theme Editor → Global → Products → Category header with an image: Banner (photo behind the title, the default) or Split (title beside the whole photo, as before). The Fashion style uses Split. See Category pages.

    Fixes¶

    Gift certificates¶

    • On-brand gift card preview: the gift card preview on the purchase and balance pages uses your theme's accent colours. It shows the amount the shopper picked, in the currency they are shopping in, instead of $0, and the To and From names update as they type.

    Mega menu¶

    • Opens smoothly: the category photo in a mega menu fades in when it loads instead of flashing in at full brightness, and moving between menu items crossfades the panels.

    Header¶

    • Cleaner search button: the search button closes off the end of the header search box neatly, without a double outline.

    Cart¶

    • Edit options window fits its content: the window for changing a cart item's options is sized to its content instead of stretching across the page; option buttons match the product page and Save spans the full width.

    1.1.7 — 22 October 2026¶

    Maintenance release. No setting or storefront text was added, removed or renamed, so installing over 1.1.6 keeps your configuration; there is nothing to configure.

    Fixes¶

    Mega menu¶

    • Page dims again under an open menu: when a mega menu panel is open, the rest of the page is dimmed again, as intended. On live stores the dimming had stopped showing.

    Filters¶

    • One scrollbar on category, brand and search pages: the filter sidebar now scrolls with the page instead of inside its own box, so a long filter list no longer shows a second scrollbar. It still stays in view while you scroll when it is short enough to fit under the header.

    Product page¶

    • Quantity stepper: the quantity is now a box with − and + buttons, and shoppers can also type a quantity. There is no longer a limit of 20; the product's own minimum and maximum purchase quantities still apply. Quick view uses the same stepper.
    • Buttons fill on hover: the wishlist, compare and Buy Now buttons and the quantity − and + buttons fill with colour when you point at them, and Add to Cart turns a shade darker.
    • Tabs no longer jump: switching between Description, Specifications and Reviews keeps the tabs where they are instead of jumping the page.
    • Payment, trust and share block: the payment icons, trust badges and share buttons line up on one left edge with even spacing, and the Print share button has a border like the others.
    • Specifications: each value now sits right beside its label instead of at the far edge of the column.

    Cart¶

    • Cart page quantity: the quantity stepper on the cart page has the same height and corner shape as the edit and remove buttons next to it.
    • Compact cart sheet: the cart sheet that slides in after Add to Cart is more compact, so a laptop screen shows three or more products at once; the promo code link stays visible.

    Food style: home page offers¶

    • Smoother offer tile shade: the darkening behind the text on the Food home page offer tiles fades out smoothly instead of ending in a visible edge.

    1.1.6 — 22 October 2026¶

    Maintenance release. No setting or storefront text was added, removed or renamed, so installing over 1.1.5 keeps your configuration; there is nothing to configure.

    Fixes¶

    Food style: home page offers¶

    • Lighter offer tiles: the two offer tiles on the Food home page are brighter. The darkening now sits only behind the text (at the bottom of the tile, and on the left of the deal tile on tablets and desktops) instead of over the whole photo, and the theme's own photos are slightly brightened. A banner image you upload yourself is shown as uploaded.

    Mobile menu¶

    • Open currency and language lists: when you open the currency or language list in the mobile menu, its arrow turns up and the list reads as one group under it. The selected currency is shown in the accent colour with a smaller check.

    Filters¶

    • No underline on tapped filter headings: on phones and tablets, a filter heading you tap (such as Rating or Price) no longer stays underlined.

    1.1.5 — 21 October 2026¶

    Installing over 1.1.4 keeps your configuration: no setting was removed or renamed, and no storefront text changed. The Food variation has new default colours (below). A store that already uses Food keeps the colours it has saved; it gets the new palette only when the Food variation is applied again with its original (default) settings, or when you set the colours yourself in Theme Styles.

    Changes¶

    Food style: olive and cream¶

    • New default palette: the Food variation now uses deep olive (#4F5B2A) for buttons, links and the navigation band, on a fresh cream page (#FBF8F1), with tomato red (#C2401C) for sale badges and the flash-sale countdown. In dark mode the accent is a sage green. Before, Food was terracotta on beige and looked close to the Fashion style. Three new Food colour defaults are set for sale and warning colours; nothing else needs configuring.

    Fixes¶

    Category pages¶

    • Compact category header with the whole photo: the category title and description sit on the left and the category image on the right, at a fixed height (240px on desktop, 200px on tablets) and at its own shape, so nothing is cut off. On phones the image sits under the title. A category without an image or description shows only what it has, without an empty panel. The title size now matches the brand pages.

    Faster images¶

    • Sized images instead of full-size originals: blog cards on the home page, the blog page and the blog post header, compare-table thumbnails and pattern swatch previews now load an image sized for where it is shown, instead of the full original upload.
    • Responsive theme images: the theme's own promo banners, Lookbook scenes and Lookbook room tiles load a size that fits the screen (400, 800 or 1200px wide, WebP where the browser supports it). Images you upload yourself are unchanged.

    Theme package¶

    • Marketplace screenshots: the Food screenshots in the theme package show the new palette.

    1.1.4 — 20 October 2026¶

    Maintenance release. No setting or storefront text was added, removed or renamed, so installing over 1.1.3 keeps your configuration; there is nothing to configure.

    Fixes¶

    Home page hero (Fashion and Food styles)¶

    • More of the hero photo on wide screens: on screens wider than about 1320px, the home page hero in the Fashion and Food styles grows taller with the width, up to the height of the window below the header, instead of staying 620px tall and cutting off the top and bottom of the photo. In the Fashion style, when the window is too short to show the whole photo, more is cut from the bottom than the top, so heads and bag handles stay in view. Phones, tablets and narrower desktops look as before.
    • Your own carousel images: when the hero shows your Control Panel carousel, the shape of the first slide's image sets how tall the hero grows; a very wide banner image keeps the 620px height.
    • Fashion: copy centred: from 801px wide, the hero headline and buttons in the Fashion style sit in the middle of the photo's height instead of near the top.
    • With a banner above: when a marketing banner or a widget sits above the hero, the hero keeps its 620px height.

    The Home style's hero is unchanged.

    1.1.3 — 20 October 2026¶

    Maintenance release. No setting or storefront text was added, removed or renamed, so installing over 1.1.2 keeps your configuration; there is nothing to configure.

    Fixes¶

    Category pages¶

    • Sharper category banner: from 600px wide, the category banner puts the title and description on a text panel and shows the category image beside it, in a box 42% of the banner's width and as tall as the banner (at least 260px, 320px on wide screens), instead of a photo stretched behind the text with most of it cut off. The image is drawn close to its own size, so it stays sharp; it is still cropped to fill its box, so a landscape image (around 3:2) with the subject in the middle works best. Phones keep the photo behind the text.
    • Same category tiles everywhere: the category tiles on the All Categories page now look the same as the home page tiles in each style. In the Fashion and Food styles that is the dark band with the white name on every photo; before, light photos on All Categories switched to a pale band with dark text.
    • Readable tile arrow: the arrow on category tiles keeps the name's colour on hover, so it stays easy to see in the Home and Fashion styles (the Food style has no arrow).
    • Filters: the Clear button in the filter sidebar appears only once a filter is set. On stores without product filtering, the price-only sidebar is more compact, and its Min field lines up with the Update button.

    Fashion style¶

    • New hero image: the second home page hero slide in the Fashion style has a new photo.

    Theme package¶

    • Image provenance: assets/PROVENANCE.md records the new hero image.

    Maintenance release. No setting or storefront text was added, removed or renamed, so installing over 1.1.1 keeps your configuration; there is nothing to configure.

    Fixes¶

    Fashion style¶

    • Option labels centred: on the product page in the Fashion style, size and colour option labels sit in the middle of their boxes instead of near the top.

    Theme package¶

    1.1.1 — 17 October 2026¶

    Maintenance release. No setting or storefront text was added, removed or renamed, so installing over 1.1.0 keeps your configuration; there is nothing to configure.

    Fixes¶

    Readability¶

    • 12px minimum text size: every text in the theme is now at least 12px at every screen width, including the "Required" note on account forms, small labels, badges, the header cart and wish list counts, and printed invoices. The cart's trust tiles keep "Secure checkout" on one line.

    Phones and tablets¶

    • Larger tap areas: on touch screens up to 1024px wide, these controls now have 44px tap areas while looking the same: the wish list remove button and wish list actions, product colour swatches (including "None"), Lookbook markers, blog tags and categories, address Edit and Delete links, account menu and order filters, search result and FAQ tabs, the header logo on tablets and the Food hero arrows. Layouts with a mouse are unchanged.

    Footer¶

    • X icon for a social link named "X": a social media link named "x" now shows the X mark in the footer, as one named "twitter" already did, instead of a generic globe.

    1.1.0 — 17 October 2026¶

    Feature release. Installing over 1.0.7 keeps your configuration: no setting was removed or renamed. Three settings are new, under Theme Styles > Products > Featured promotions, and start with the values below. Two new storefront texts ship translated in every language file.

    New setting Default
    Show featured promotions (show_featured_promotions) On
    Promotion background (yen-offer-bg-color) Home #FFF7ED, Fashion #F7F3EC, Food #FBEFE6
    Promotion text (yen-offer-text-color) Home #B84600, Fashion #7A5D33, Food #8C3B26

    New features¶

    Featured Promotions¶

    • Promotion callouts on products: when you feature an automatic promotion and give it a callout, the callout shows under the price on product cards (category, brand, search, home sections, related products, quick search), on the product page and in quick view. Cards show the top promotion and "+N more offers"; the product page and quick view list every one. Turn it off, or change its colours, in Theme Styles > Products > Featured promotions. Dark mode builds the callout colours from your Dark Mode Colors.
    • BigCommerce rollout: BigCommerce is enabling featured promotion data store by store. Until it is on for your store, no callouts appear, and nothing else changes.

    Backorders¶

    • Backorders on the product page and in quick view: a product that can be backordered keeps Add to Cart when nothing is on hand (it is no longer labelled Sold Out), shows the availability prompt, how many units will be backordered and the product's backorder message (or your store's default message), and, for pick-list options, each option's own lines. A quantity above what can be sold disables Add to Cart with "The maximum purchasable quantity is N", also per pick-list option. The "Get it by" delivery estimate is withdrawn while any unit is backordered.
    • Backorders in the cart and orders: cart lines, in the cart page and the cart panel, show how many units are ready to ship, how many will be backordered and the backorder message, with a group per pick-list option, and the cart summary adds your shipping expectation when the cart holds a backordered item. Order details in My Account show the same lines under each product and the shipping expectation under the shipping address. What shows follows your Settings > Inventory choices. Backorder quantities appear only when Settings > Inventory > Stock level display is not "Don't show": BigCommerce sends no backorder figures while stock levels are hidden.

    Fixes¶

    Product page¶

    • Sold-out variant after an in-stock one: choosing a sold-out variant after an in-stock one now disables Add to Cart and shows Sold Out; the earlier variant's stock figure no longer carries over. A quick view opened over a product page reads its own product's stock, not the page product's.

    Shipping¶

    • No free-shipping promise next to a fixed shipping cost: a product with its own fixed shipping cost no longer shows the free-shipping bar on the product page; the line by Add to Cart states the cost instead. In the cart page and the cart panel, the free-shipping progress and "FREE" are left out when every product in the cart has a fixed shipping cost.

    Tablets¶

    • Larger tap areas on touch tablets: on tablets (768 to 1024px wide, touch screens), footer links, social icons, pagination, brand letters, grid/list toggles, the top-bar currency and light/dark buttons, product option chips and Lookbook markers have 44px tap areas. Layouts with a mouse are unchanged; the footer gets a little taller on tablets.

    Theme package¶

    • Image provenance: the theme package includes assets/PROVENANCE.md, which records how each bundled and demo image was made and its licence terms.

    1.0.7 — 16 October 2026¶

    Maintenance release. No setting or storefront text was added, removed or renamed, so installing over 1.0.6 keeps your configuration; there is nothing to configure.

    Fixes¶

    Account¶

    • Wishlist Add to Cart readable in dark mode: on a wishlist page in dark mode, the Add to Cart button label was dark on the dark card and almost invisible, and in light mode its outline was very faint. The label, the outline and the hover fill now follow the colour mode. The Pre-order button and the stock note below it are easier to read too.

    1.0.6 — 16 October 2026¶

    Maintenance release. No setting was added, removed or renamed, so installing over 1.0.5 keeps your configuration. Two new storefront texts, the promo code links in the cart panel below, ship translated in every language file; there is nothing to configure.

    Fixes¶

    Account¶

    • Wishlist cards show the product again: on a wishlist page, the product photo no longer covers the card, so the name, the price and the Add to Cart button are visible and can be tapped.
    • Clearer "Required" labels: the "Required" note next to form fields is 12px at every screen width, the same as on phones.

    Currency¶

    • Switching currency without a cart: choosing another currency before anything is in the cart now switches the store currency straight away, with no error in the browser console. With items in the cart, the cart changes to the new currency as before.
    • Fashion currency button: in the Fashion style, the currency button in the top bar is as large and easy to tap as in the Home style.

    Cart¶

    • Promo codes in the cart panel: the cart panel now shows a link, "Have a promo code? Apply it in your cart" (or "promo code or gift card" when gift certificates are on), that opens the cart page, instead of a field that could not be typed in.
    • Easier quantity buttons: the - and + buttons in the cart panel have a larger tap area on phones and tablets, and with a mouse on small laptops; they look the same.
    • Remove confirmation in the theme style: removing an item from the cart panel asks for confirmation in the theme's own dialog, the same one the cart page uses, instead of the browser's plain pop-up. Pressing Escape closes the dialog and leaves the cart panel open.

    Food style¶

    • Readable reviews in dark mode: in the Food style in dark mode, the review date and the "Verified" badge on home page testimonials have stronger contrast.

    Pages¶

    • Newsletter confirmation pages: the pages shown after subscribing, unsubscribing or creating an account no longer show a white band above the content panel.
    • Sale link: when no promo banner link is set, the home page promo button opens /sale/ directly, without a redirect.

    1.0.5 — 15 October 2026¶

    Maintenance release. No setting was added, removed or renamed, so installing over 1.0.4 keeps your configuration. One new storefront text, the "Added to cart" heading below, ships translated in every language file; there is nothing to configure.

    Fixes¶

    Phones¶

    • Newsletter sign-up in one row: in the footer and the home page newsletter band, the email field and its subscribe button now sit in a single row, with an arrow button inside the end of the field, instead of a field stacked over a full-width button. Tablets and desktops look as before.
    • Sign in first in the mobile menu: at the bottom of the menu drawer, Sign in is the filled main button and Register (Log out when signed in) the outlined one, and the drawer no longer ends in a band of empty space.
    • Closer hero slide dots: the slide dots on the home page hero are larger and closer together, each still easy to tap, and the pause button is easier to hit. Tablets and desktops look as before.
    • Calmer tap feedback: tapping a link or button no longer flashes a grey square over rounded chips and tiles. Category chips, category tiles and hero buttons show a brief colour change when pressed instead.

    Cart¶

    • "Added to cart" after adding: the cart panel that opens after Add to Cart is headed "Added to cart" with a check mark, so shoppers see at once that the item went in. Opened from the cart icon in the header it still reads "Your Cart".

    Product images¶

    • Image backgrounds blend in light mode: product photos on cards, in Quick View, on the product page and in the cart sit on a soft background matched to each style (Home, Fashion, Food), so photos shot on an off-white studio background no longer show as a separate pale box. Dark mode, banners, lookbook, category and brand images are unchanged.

    1.0.4 — 15 October 2026¶

    Maintenance release. No setting was added, removed or renamed, so installing over 1.0.3 keeps your configuration.

    Fixes¶

    Header¶

    1.0.0 — Initial release¶

    Yen Theme for BigCommerce, built on Cornerstone 6.19.0.

    Variations¶

    Three, with the same features. Each has its own layout preset (Theme Editor → Yen Theme → Layout Preset) as well as its own colours and type.

    • Home — Classic layout, the theme's own defaults
    • Fashion — Editorial layout: centred logo, full-bleed hero, display serif, brass accent, opens in dark mode
    • Food — Market layout: search-first header, round category tags, five-across home grids, herb green, its own photographs and copy

    Shopping experience¶

    • Home hero shows your Storefront → Carousel slides when you add them, in the theme's own hero design; without slides it shows the theme's three built-in slides
    • Dark/light mode toggle, with both modes designed rather than inverted
    • Mega navigation with featured images and multi-column layouts
    • Product cards with quick view, wishlist, compare and hover detail
    • Grid and list view on category, brand and search pages
    • Faceted search with a sidebar on desktop and a drawer on mobile
    • Cart page with a summary sidebar, shipping estimator and gift certificates

    The Lookbook¶

    Shoppable images: place hotspots on a photograph as percentage coordinates and map each one to a product ID. 41 settings, and a page template of its own.

    Custom page templates¶

    Four, each chosen from the template dropdown when creating a web page: Lookbook, About, FAQ and Track Order. All four render their own content — the page body you type is not what appears. See The other page templates for what that means in practice.

    Homepage¶

    Hero banner, trust badges, brand showcase, flash sale, reviews, press mentions and newsletter, each with its own on/off switch in the Theme Editor.

    Under the hood¶

    • Full Page Builder and Theme Editor integration — 380 settings across 9 sections
    • Lazy loading with low-quality image placeholders, so images resolve in rather than jump
    • Responsive from phone to desktop, with the desktop layout starting at 1261px
    • Accessibility: ARIA attributes throughout, focus trapping in overlays, keyboard navigation, named landmarks, and a single main landmark per page
    • 20 storefront locales covering 13 distinct translations

    Phones and tablets¶

    • Every page reworked for phones, tablets and phone landscape, in both colour modes, and readable at up to 200% text size
    • Add to Cart opens the cart as a sheet over the page instead of leaving the product
    • A per-slide Hero Focus (phones & tablets) setting keeps each hero photograph's subject in view when the picture is cropped — see Hero focus
    • One button system per layout preset, with the hover and pressed shades mixed from the Button colour

    Cart¶

    • The cart page, the cart sheet and the product page say how much more qualifies for free shipping, in the shopper's currency
    • The cart page's cross-sell row leaves out products already in the cart
    • A promotion that splits a discounted unit onto its own line shows as one product, and minimum and maximum purchase quantities count across both lines

    Prices hidden from guests¶

    When the store hides prices from guests, no price reaches the page: not on product cards, bulk pricing tables, the product page's fixed shipping cost, the free-shipping bar, or the structured data search engines read. See Prices hidden from guests for what that means for Google rich results.

    Upgrading from a pre-release build¶

    • Fonts: pick your body and heading fonts again so every weight loads — see Typography.
    • Primary button colour: #1B1D21, #333333 and #000000 in the Cornerstone primary button controls read as "not set" — see Primary button colour.

    What the theme does not do¶

    • No external services and no API keys. Everything rendered comes from your own catalog and settings.
    • No order-lookup form on Track Order. BigCommerce provides no storefront endpoint a theme can query for a guest's order, so the page offers what does work: order history for signed-in shoppers, and the emailed tracking link for guests.
