    Release Notes¶

    1.1.40 — 29 October 2026¶

    No Theme Editor setting was added, removed or renamed.

    Fixes¶

    • Product photos fill the frame again. 1.1.39 showed every product page photo whole, which left empty bands beside photos a little narrower than the frame (every Fashion photo, and tall lifestyle shots on Home). The photo now fills the frame; only one much wider or taller than the frame (more than one and a half times, such as a banner-shaped photo) is shown whole. Thumbnails fill their tiles.
    • Fashion style: the product photo frame is 3:4, the shape of the Fashion photos and product cards, so they show uncropped.

    1.1.39 — 29 October 2026¶

    No Theme Editor setting was added, removed or renamed.

    Fixes¶

    • Whole product photo on the product page. Photos that are not square (wide shots, tall bottles) are fitted inside the gallery frame instead of cropped to a square, in the main image and the thumbnails.
    • Add to Cart says Sold Out for a sold-out choice. Picking an option that is out of stock turns the button label to Sold Out; picking an in-stock option brings the label back.
    • Gift wrapping line reads as information. "Gift wrapping: Options available" is plain text, as in Cornerstone, instead of a label that looked like an option to click; the wrap is still chosen in the cart.

    1.1.38 — 29 October 2026¶

    No Theme Editor setting was added, removed or renamed.

    Fixes¶

    • One ring round the chosen colour. On the product page the selected colour swatch showed three circles (a thin dark ring, the accent border and a faint glow under the pointer). It now shows one accent ring with a small gap, as in Quick View; hovering another swatch only tints its border.

    1.1.37 — 29 October 2026¶

    No Theme Editor setting was added, removed or renamed. One style default changed: the Fashion style now opens category pages with the photo banner (Category header style: Banner), like Home and Food. Pick Split in Theme Editor to keep the photo beside the title.

    Fixes¶

    • Food style: the trust strip under the hero shows each label above its text, so the three claims line up even when one text runs to two lines.
    • Fashion style: category banner photos are cropped from nearer the top, keeping collars and handles in view.

    1.1.36 — 29 October 2026¶

    No Theme Editor setting was added, removed or renamed.

    Fixes¶

    • Product details stay put when an option is picked. The SKU (and UPC) now sit on their own line, so choosing a colour or size that changes the SKU no longer pushes Weight sideways.
    • Fashion style: the hero button labels and arrows sit in the middle of their buttons (they rode a few pixels high).

    1.1.35 — 28 October 2026¶

    No Theme Editor setting was added, removed or renamed. The theme keeps showing only what your store's own data backs: a few more defaults that promised a returns policy, a sale deadline or member perks now ship empty. Settings you have already saved in Theme Editor keep your values.

    Fixes¶

    Home page and promises¶

    • No returns promise you did not make. The top bar, hero and footer pills, cart trust badges, home trust rows and the product page trust list no longer say "30-day returns" by default. To show your own returns policy, set Hero pills to Your text and fill in Pill 2 (and the top bar message 2) in Theme Editor.
    • Sale banner only when there is a sale. The promo banner (and the Food style's deal tile) shows when you set its link or your store has a "Sale" category; otherwise it stays hidden instead of sending shoppers to all products.
    • Flash Sale countdown only with your date. There is no default end date any more, so the countdown and "Hurry up" line appear only once you set Flash Sale End Date.
    • The home newsletter text no longer promises early access or members-only discounts; your own newsletter text still takes its place.
    • Trust badges fill the row whatever their number; a short Shop by Category row starts under its heading; Recently Viewed prices line up; on phones the product tabs stay on one line.

    Product cards, product page and compare¶

    • No "Add to Cart" on products you do not sell online. A product with purchasing disabled or a hidden price shows "View details" on cards and in the wishlist.
    • Stock label tells the truth. A product with stock that cannot be bought online shows no stock label instead of "Out of stock"; an empty "Current Stock:" line is gone.
    • Sold out cards use a crossed-circle icon instead of a notification bell.
    • Compare shows "In stock" (or "Pre-order") for products you sell without stock tracking, instead of "N/A".
    • Fashion style: option buttons keep your text's capitals ("Intel Core i5"), and the product photo area in dark mode is no longer a bright white block.
    • On phones the menu button sits a little further from the logo.

    1.1.34 — 28 October 2026¶

    No Theme Editor setting was added, removed or renamed. The theme now shows only what your store's own data backs: out of the box it shows no sample content and makes no shipping promise. To get there some defaults changed (free-shipping messages, testimonials, About page figures and values, Lookbook products and links). Settings you have already saved in Theme Editor keep your values.

    Fixes¶

    Home, About and FAQ pages¶

    • No shipping promise you did not make. The free-shipping progress messages are off by default, so the top bar, product page, cart, hero pills and footer no longer promise free shipping over an amount your store never set. Turn the messages on and set your threshold in Theme Editor > Cart when your shipping rules offer it. The shipping trust tile now reads "Shipping Options".
    • No sample content by default. Testimonials, the About page team, figures and values, and the brand story figures no longer appear with made-up names and numbers; each shows once you fill it in. The FAQ page no longer tells search engines about its sample answers, and the About page's built-in sentences no longer mention fast shipping or "thousands of products".
    • Home sections follow your products. Flash Sale lists only products that really have a discount (and hides when none do); the promo banner button opens your Sale category, or no button shows; the classic hero without your own slides shows one general slide; the blog button shows only when your store has a blog; Recently Viewed follows its setting and count.

    Cart, drawer and product page¶

    • Totals you can trust. The cart drawer total now matches the cart page (gift wrapping and tax included), and neither shows "FREE" shipping or "You've unlocked free shipping" while BigCommerce says the order still needs to qualify.
    • Out-of-stock choices can't be bought. When the chosen options are out of stock, Buy Now, the sticky Add to Cart and the free-shipping note step aside with Add to Cart, and the "In stock" label turns to "Out of stock".
    • No invented delivery date. The "Get it by ..." line, which no store data backed, is gone.
    • The buying column stays beside a long description on desktop, the sticky bar shows the price once, and a product with no reviews says so above Write a Review.

    Product cards, quick view, compare and wishlist¶

    • "Hide prices from guests" and "Show product rating" are respected on every card. With prices hidden, guests see "Log in for pricing" instead of an empty price row; with ratings off, cards show no stars.
    • Quick view: a price range only when it has both ends, whole photos on the card's own background in dark mode, and room around the options and buttons.
    • Cards: "On Sale!" for any real discount, "Sold Out" links to the product instead of a Notify Me button that did nothing, and titles line up when only some products have a brand.
    • Compare: no "Best Value" tag the data did not back, and availability matches the cards. Wishlist and compare photos show whole.

    Navigation and listings for small stores¶

    • The Shop menu panel drops when it would only repeat a single category; the Brands link hides when the store has no brands, and appears in the phone menu.
    • Category filters: the first four groups open, the rest collapsed; Clear shows only while a filter is on; a search with no results has no filter column.
    • Blog with no posts shows an empty message; a Lookbook look whose products cannot load says so.

    1.1.33 — 28 October 2026¶

    No setting was added, removed or renamed, so installing over 1.1.32 keeps your configuration.

    Fixes¶

    Home page¶

    • "View all" links on every product section. Flash Sale, New Arrivals, Best Sellers and the "Our Products" tabs showed a "View all" link only when your store had a category named exactly like the demo stores ("Sale", "New Arrivals", "Best Sellers", "Featured" or "Shop All"). When a section has no category of its own, its "View all" now opens your store-wide category, sorted to match the section (newest, best selling or featured): a category named "Shop All", or one whose name starts with "Shop All" (such as "Shop All Store 2"), or your only top-level category if you have just one. A store with none of these shows no link, as before. A category named like the section still comes first. See Homepage.

    1.1.32 — 28 October 2026¶

    No setting was added, removed or renamed, so installing over 1.1.31 keeps your configuration. Stores whose product photos are the shape of the card (square, or 3:4 on the Fashion style) see the same cards as before.

    Fixes¶

    Product cards¶

    • The whole photo, never cropped. A product photo of another shape than the card (a wide sofa in a square card) lost its sides. Cards on category, search and brand pages, the home page rows and carousels, and Recently Viewed now show the whole photo on the card's light ground. Square photos (or 3:4 photos on the Fashion style) fill the card as before; the second photo shown on hover still fills it.

    Product page¶

    • No empty space under the photo. A product with many options made a tall buying column, and the description tabs started only where it ended, leaving a large gap under the photo and another under the tabs. The tabs and videos now follow the photo directly. Phones are unchanged: photo, buying column, then tabs.
    • Hidden "__" custom fields. Custom fields whose name starts with two underscores (__), as used by apps and for settings, no longer appear in the Specifications tab or the compare page. Name a field that way to keep it off the storefront. See Product pages.
    • Long specification values stay in their column. A long value such as a web address wraps inside its own cell instead of running over the next specification.
    • The reviews count matches the reviews shown. The Reviews tab, the "N reviews" link and the review summary count the reviews the tab lists. BigCommerce can count reviews it does not show (for example reviews not approved for this storefront), which made "Reviews (6)" open an empty tab.
    • Recently Viewed matches Related Products. The Recently Viewed row uses the same card size, columns and type as Related Products (four across from tablets up). The home page's Recently Viewed row is unchanged.
    • Buttons in your description respond to the pointer. Links styled as theme buttons (class button) in a product description or warranty fill with your accent tint on hover, like the product page's own buttons.

    Blog¶

    • "← Blog" goes back to the blog. The link under a blog post reloaded the post instead of opening the blog. It now opens your blog's own address on each storefront.

    Menu¶

    • Shop menu tiles in your colours. A category tile without an image in the Shop menu showed an orange tint on hover with the Fashion and Food styles; it now uses your accent colour, and the tile name has more room underneath.

    1.1.31 — 28 October 2026¶

    No setting was added, removed or renamed, so installing over 1.1.30 keeps your configuration. With the default settings every page shows the same as before. Two help texts in Theme Editor > Ember Theme > Homepage are updated: the Hero and footer pills note and the Pill 2 label.

    Fixes¶

    FAQ page¶

    • The FAQ template shows your own questions and answers. A Web Page using the FAQ template always showed the thirteen sample questions, whatever you wrote in the page. The text you write in the page (Storefront > Web Pages) now appears under the FAQ banner, which takes the page's name as its heading; the sample questions, their search box, category filter and search-engine data are left out. A page with no text still shows the samples, as before. See Page templates.

    Cart page¶

    • The returns badge follows your returns pill. The "30-day returns" badge in the cart page's trust row could not be changed. It now follows Hero and footer pills: with Built-in text it reads "30-day returns" as before; with Your text below it reads your Pill 2, so one edit changes the returns promise in the hero, the footer and the cart; an empty Pill 2, or Hidden, removes the badge and the row closes up.

    Home page and footer¶

    • Your free-shipping amount in your own pills, in the shopper's currency. An amount typed into a pill ("Free shipping over $150") showed $150 to a shopper browsing in euros. Type {amount} instead ("Free shipping over {amount}"): the pill shows your Free Shipping ($) amount, converted to the shopper's currency like the top bar's default message. While Free Shipping Messages is off or the amount is 0, a pill containing {amount} is hidden. See Hero pills.

    1.1.30 — 27 October 2026¶

    No setting was added, removed or renamed, so installing over 1.1.29 keeps your configuration; there is nothing to configure.

    Fixes¶

    Home page¶

    • Promo banner badges stay lined up when Free Shipping Messages is off. Under the home promo banner, the shipping badge has no threshold note when Free Shipping Messages is off or the amount is 0. The badges after it then slid one place, so a returns note sat beside the wrong title and the next badge started a line late. Where the badges stack in rows, each badge now starts its own row with its title and note beside its icon, whether or not the shipping note shows.

    1.1.29 — 27 October 2026¶

    One setting was added and none removed or renamed, so installing over 1.1.28 keeps your configuration. With the default settings every page shows the same text as before. One correction applies on upgrade: the Account created page now states your own Free Shipping ($) amount instead of a fixed $50, and drops that line if Free Shipping Messages is off or the amount is 0.

    Fixes¶

    Shipping and returns promises¶

    • Footer pills follow the hero pills. The three pills in the footer ("Free shipping", "Secure checkout", "30-day returns") could not be changed. They now follow Theme Editor > Ember Theme > Homepage > Hero and footer pills (formerly Hero pills): Built-in text shows them as before, Your text below shows your Pill 1 to Pill 3 (an empty pill is hidden), Hidden removes them. See Hero pills.
    • Account created page: your own free-shipping amount. The third perk said "Enjoy free shipping on your first order over $50" whatever your store charges. It now states your Free Shipping ($) amount, in the shopper's currency on a multi-currency store, and is left out when Free Shipping Messages is off or the amount is 0, the same rule as the top bar and the cart.
    • First hero slide no longer promises shipping on its own. The theme's own first slide (classic look) said "... with fast shipping and hassle-free returns." When you choose Your text below or Hidden for the pills, that slide now reads "Browse our curated collection of trending products." With Built-in text it is unchanged. The Fashion and Food slides never mentioned shipping.
    • Promo banner returns note can be edited. Under the home promo banner, the returns badge's note "Hassle free returns" came from the language files. Promo returns note (after Promo Trust Badges) replaces it with your own text; leave it empty to keep the built-in note.

    1.1.28 — 27 October 2026¶

    New settings were added and none removed or renamed, so installing over 1.1.27 keeps your configuration. Out of the box the home page hero shows the same three pills as before; nothing changes until you choose your own text.

    New¶

    Home page¶

    • Hero pills: your own text, or none. In Theme Editor > Ember Theme > Homepage, Hero pills controls the three short notes under the hero buttons ("Free shipping", "30-day returns", "Secure checkout"). Choose Your text below to reword them in Pill 1 to Pill 3 (leave a pill empty to hide it), or Hidden to remove them. If your store charges shipping on some or all orders, change or hide the "Free shipping" pill. The same choice applies when the hero is built from your Control Panel carousel slides. See Hero pills.

    Fixes¶

    Blog¶

    • Blog cards line up. On the blog page and in the home page blog section, a post's tag now sits on the top-left corner of its photo instead of above the title, so every card's title starts at the same height. The author and date row and "Read more" sit at the bottom of each card, so a short excerpt no longer leaves them floating. A post without a thumbnail shows a soft placeholder in your accent colour, in the same frame as a photo, instead of an empty box; the featured post at the top of the blog page does the same.

    1.1.27 — 27 October 2026¶

    No setting was added, removed or renamed, so installing over 1.1.26 keeps your configuration. Out of the box every page looks as it did; the free-shipping messages change only if you turn them off or set the amount to 0.

    Fixes¶

    Cart and shipping¶

    • Free-shipping messages can be turned off everywhere.     1.0.0 — Initial release¶

    Yen Theme for BigCommerce, built on Cornerstone 6.19.0.

    Variations¶

    Three, with the same features. Each has its own layout preset (Theme Editor → Yen Theme → Layout Preset) as well as its own colours and type.

    • Home — Classic layout, the theme's own defaults
    • Fashion — Editorial layout: centred logo, full-bleed hero, display serif, brass accent, opens in dark mode
    • Food — Market layout: search-first header, round category tags, five-across home grids, herb green, its own photographs and copy

    Shopping experience¶

    • Home hero shows your Storefront → Carousel slides when you add them, in the theme's own hero design; without slides it shows the theme's three built-in slides
    • Dark/light mode toggle, with both modes designed rather than inverted
    • Mega navigation with featured images and multi-column layouts
    • Product cards with quick view, wishlist, compare and hover detail
    • Grid and list view on category, brand and search pages
    • Faceted search with a sidebar on desktop and a drawer on mobile
    • Cart page with a summary sidebar, shipping estimator and gift certificates

    The Lookbook¶

    Shoppable images: place hotspots on a photograph as percentage coordinates and map each one to a product ID. 41 settings, and a page template of its own.

    Custom page templates¶

    Four, each chosen from the template dropdown when creating a web page: Lookbook, About, FAQ and Track Order. All four render their own content — the page body you type is not what appears. See The other page templates for what that means in practice.

    Homepage¶

    Hero banner, trust badges, brand showcase, flash sale, reviews, press mentions and newsletter, each with its own on/off switch in the Theme Editor.

    Under the hood¶

    • Full Page Builder and Theme Editor integration — 380 settings across 9 sections
    • Lazy loading with low-quality image placeholders, so images resolve in rather than jump
    • Responsive from phone to desktop, with the desktop layout starting at 1261px
    • Accessibility: ARIA attributes throughout, focus trapping in overlays, keyboard navigation, named landmarks, and a single main landmark per page
    • 20 storefront locales covering 13 distinct translations

    Phones and tablets¶

    • Every page reworked for phones, tablets and phone landscape, in both colour modes, and readable at up to 200% text size
    • Add to Cart opens the cart as a sheet over the page instead of leaving the product
    • A per-slide Hero Focus (phones & tablets) setting keeps each hero photograph's subject in view when the picture is cropped — see Hero focus
    • One button system per layout preset, with the hover and pressed shades mixed from the Button colour

    Cart¶

    • The cart page, the cart sheet and the product page say how much more qualifies for free shipping, in the shopper's currency
    • The cart page's cross-sell row leaves out products already in the cart
    • A promotion that splits a discounted unit onto its own line shows as one product, and minimum and maximum purchase quantities count across both lines

    Prices hidden from guests¶

    When the store hides prices from guests, no price reaches the page: not on product cards, bulk pricing tables, the product page's fixed shipping cost, the free-shipping bar, or the structured data search engines read. See Prices hidden from guests for what that means for Google rich results.

    Upgrading from a pre-release build¶

    • Fonts: pick your body and heading fonts again so every weight loads — see Typography.
    • Primary button colour: #1B1D21, #333333 and #000000 in the Cornerstone primary button controls read as "not set" — see Primary button colour.

    What the theme does not do¶

    • No external services and no API keys. Everything rendered comes from your own catalog and settings.
    • No order-lookup form on Track Order. BigCommerce provides no storefront endpoint a theme can query for a guest's order, so the page offers what does work: order history for signed-in shoppers, and the emailed tracking link for guests.
